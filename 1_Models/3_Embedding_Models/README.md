# 3. Embedding Models

## What is an Embedding?
An embedding is a way to convert text (words, sentences, or entire documents) into a list of numbers (a vector). 

Why is this useful? Because computers don't understand words, they understand math. By turning sentences into numbers, we can plot them in a mathematical space. Sentences that mean similar things will be grouped close together, while unrelated sentences will be far apart.

## What's in this folder?
- **1_embed_queries.py**: Shows how to embed a single, short query (like a user's search term).
- **2_embed_documents.py**: Shows how to embed multiple, longer documents (like paragraphs from a book or a database of information).

By mastering embeddings, you can search through thousands of documents for the *meaning* of a word, not just an exact keyword match!
